To My Dearest Kanna, My Lil Pumpkin Always & Forever Yours 🕊️

I love you more than words could ever describe, my beautiful rose. Yet, looking back at these past 4 years, my biggest regret in this life is how blindly I treated the only woman whose heart beat for me.

You stood by me with endless loyalty, Kanna. You gently tried to help me change, you waited with infinite patience, and you asked for nothing more than to be valued, cherished, and loved. But I was foolish. I gave my attention, my time, and my care to everyone in the world around me—friends, outsiders, distractions—except to my lil pumpkin whose happiness should have been my entire universe.

When you finally burst, it shattered my ignorance. Knowing that my Kanao spent sleepless nights crying alone in the dark, feeling unprioritized, unvalued, and neglected because of my foolishness tears my soul into pieces. You did not deserve a single tear of that sorrow.

I am truly, deeply sorry, my beautiful rose. I regret every single second I made you feel second best. You are my dream, my home, and the absolute love of my life. I will never take your heart for granted again.
